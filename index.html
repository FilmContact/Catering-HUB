Privacy Policy

CaterMatch helps connect catering workers and event employers. We collect profile and booking information needed to operate the platform.

  • Profiles are reviewed before they are made available for platform use.
  • Users should provide accurate contact, location and profile information.
  • Location coordinates are used to show approximate distance for nearby matching.
  • Messages and booking information are used to support communication and platform coordination.
  • Payments are strictly between the Worker and the Employer. CaterMatch does not collect or process work payments.
  • CaterMatch does not charge brokerage or commission on work arranged directly between workers and employers through the platform.
Use CaterMatch to connect directly and avoid brokerage/commission charges. Agree on payment, timing and other work terms directly with the other party before accepting an event.
